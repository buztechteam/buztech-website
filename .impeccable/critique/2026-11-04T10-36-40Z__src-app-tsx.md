## Design Specificity Verdict

What is product-specific lives in the copy and the price-request flow, not the visuals.

## Priority Issues

1. [P0] The price-request form does not deliver. The live bundle has no Web3Forms key, so "Get a price" opens the visitor's email app; a buyer on a locked-down PC or webmail hits a dead end. Fix: create the Web3Forms key and set VITE_WEB3FORMS_KEY; until then, make the success/error state lead with WhatsApp and the phone. /impeccable harden
2. [P1] The form fields don't match the promise. "What happens next" says we contact by phone or email, but there is no phone field; required/optional fields are unmarked; errors do not clear as they are fixed. Fix: optional phone field, marked optional fields, live error clearing, stronger in-palette error state. /impeccable clarify
3. [P1] Generic visual language. Page intros are empty gradient bands (right ~60% bare on desktop); nothing visual says "licences at the best price". Fix: one premium, product-specific object within the brand rules (e.g. what every order includes drawn as the anatomy of a quote). /impeccable shape, then /impeccable layout
